Guesswhat Studio started as an indie game studio. It still is, in the important sense: we care about rules, surprise, pacing, mood, and interfaces that invite people to play.

The first public releases happen to be tools, because some sparks want to become tools before they become games. The through-line is the same craft: small systems, sharp interaction design, strange premises, and enough randomness to make discovery feel alive.

Our Chinese name, 全靠猜, is a useful compass. The dice logo is not randomness for its own sake; it is a bet on playful uncertainty. Let chance open a door, then use taste and design to make the room worth entering.

## What We Care About

- Play first: even non-game tools should have rhythm, agency, and a sense of discovery.
- Strong premises: a project starts when an idea has enough spark to survive implementation.
- Useful uncertainty: randomness can be an input, but taste is the filter.
- Small systems: clear rules, visible state, good defaults, and escape hatches.
- Founder-level maintenance: the work is opinionated, hands-on, and shaped by direct use.
